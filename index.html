Who we are

A festival with purpose.

Muntopia Fest is an entertainment and events initiative bringing people together through music, culture, family activities and shared experiences. We aim to create opportunities for young people, showcase talent and build meaningful connections between communities, businesses and the creative industry.

Our vision

To grow a recognisable events brand that delivers memorable experiences while creating positive community impact.

Our mission

To provide a welcoming platform for entertainment, local talent, youth participation and partnerships that help communities thrive.
